HIMMEL · MK2

The MK2 in Himmel

Cheat sheet and audience view: what every control does in PERFORM. Shown full screen on a second monitor if you like.
Green = prepare (PREVIEW)
Left screen, knobs 1–4, top-row groups A–D, top buttons 1–4 (what the dice may change).
Red = live (PROGRAM)
Right screen, knobs 5–8, bottom-row groups E–H, top buttons 5–8 (freeze, strobe, black, assist).
The wheel is the crossfader. Turn = blend PREVIEW into PROGRAM; one turn = done and the screens swap roles. Push or PLAY = automatic TAKE. RESTART = cut.
Pads = clips. Thumbnails in the GUI, the clip's colour on the pad. Tap = load into the selected layer, press harder = squeeze its effect. Other pad modes: LOOKS, FX PADS, OUTPUTS.
Dice: knob 2 or transport ◀ ▶. REC saves the look to a pad.
