What projector 2 shows right now
A big crosshair and the mask outlines are projected onto the real stage while you draw. Put the crosshair on the real corner, click, next corner. What you see on the stage is what you get.
With the hardware
Block: a trackpad for the crosshair (absolute X/Y on the output). Tap = add a point, MODE = close the mask.
MK2: knobs 1 / 2 = move the selected point X / Y (fine, SHIFT = finer), pads = pick a point, REC = add, ERASE = delete point, wheel = zoom the view.
