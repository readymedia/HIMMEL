How it feels
1. Select layer A (group A). Turn knob 2: new looks roll in on PREVIEW.
2. Like it? REC, it is now a LAYER preset (and in history with a star).
3. Press ALL, turn knob 2 with SHIFT: rotate through your saved LOOKS.
4. Wheel or PLAY to take it live. Turn on auto-rotate for a song you don't want to babysit.
Rules
· Levels nest: a LOOK holds LAYER presets, a LAYER holds EFFECT presets, so saving the look keeps all of it.
· Rotate and dice respect the locks (Top 1–4) and never break the GPU budget.
· Auto-rotate lands on the beat / phrase, respects GRID and stops when you touch anything on that level.
· Every preset gets an automatic name (mood + palette + number) and a thumbnail.
