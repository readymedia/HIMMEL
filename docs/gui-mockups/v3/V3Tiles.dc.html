No knobs on screen. Tiles that show what turning will do.

Each tile sits exactly above its physical knob. The middle is now; the faded edges are what you get if you turn left or right. The thin bar is the value. Under the PREVIEW screen they change the preview; under the PROGRAM screen they change what is live.
SCROLLKNOB 1 · preview
A film strip. Turn = the next clip slides in and loads into the selected preview layer at once. Safe: it is only preview.
DICEKNOB 2 · preview
NEW
A deck of cards. Right = roll a new look (inside the mood, with your locks). Left = back through history. Same as transport ◀ ▶.
EFFECT AMOUNTKNOB 3 · preview
Before ◀ now ▶ more. The tile is the effect itself on your clip, so you see kaleido get more segments as you turn.
SPEEDKNOB 4 · preview
×1.5½ · 1 · 1½ · 2 · 4 · on the beat
Streaks stretch with speed. Steps snap to musical values when GRID is on; Shift + turn = free.
INTENSITYKNOB 5 · program
The energy macro on what is live: speed, contrast, cut rate, strobe level, all at once, within limits that look good.
COLOURKNOB 6 · program
A colour ring around the live picture. Turn = the palette turns. Pushed fully left = the clips' own colours.
SPACEKNOB 7 · program
Left = mirror and tiles, middle = flat, right = tunnel and zoom. One knob for the "space" of the picture.
MASTER FXKNOB 8 · program
dryGLOW 40 %
A wipe: left of the line is without, right is with. Turn = move the line. Shift + turn = choose the master effect.
