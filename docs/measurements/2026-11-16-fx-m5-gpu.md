# M5 effects and generators: GPU time per pass (2026-11-16)

- **Machine:** the owner's dev laptop, NVIDIA GeForce GTX 1070, Windows 11.
- **Method:** `build\bin\Release\fx_check.exe --draws 400 --md ...`. Each
  effect is one full-screen pass at **1920×1080** (RGBA8), timed with D3D11
  timestamp queries around 400 draws (after 5 warm-up draws), once at the
  effect's defaults and once at its `extreme` preset. Effects read a
  synthetic test picture; the flash gate is on; trails read a canvas-sized
  texture as its feedback.
- **Conditions:** no other Himmel renderer running (a first run while another
  agent's `himmel_render` was active read 30–50 % higher; those numbers were
  thrown away).
- **Classes** (`design/effects-and-generators.md` §2): S < 0.15 ms,
  M < 0.6 ms. `fx_check` exits 1 if an effect is over its class.

Sum of all 25 at their defaults: 2.49 ms (one of each; a real show runs a few
at a time). A plain full-screen copy costs about 0.04 ms on this card, so most
S effects are within 2× of the fill-rate floor.

Notes:
- **Tile** at 8 tiles minifies the picture 8× without mips: texture-cache
  bound (0.31 ms), so it is class M (the design table says S–M).
- **Blur** (round, 16 taps) costs more at its default size than the extreme
  preset (a 24-tap line): round taps spread over more cache lines.
- **Glow** at its largest size dropped from 0.99 ms to 0.30 ms by removing the
  per-pixel random turn of its tap pattern (neighbouring pixels then share
  texels). Its largest size is 4 % of the height.
- **Noise** (3 octaves + a warp) and **Shapes** (up to 6 polygon outlines) are
  ALU-bound generators, class M.
- Shader compile time at start-up (25 shaders, worker threads): 180–230 ms.

## In the engine (same day)

`himmel_render --outputs 1 --size 960x540 --flash-cap 3 --test-signal`, with
kaleido + trails on a HAP clip, ticker and rings generators on empty layers,
glow on deck A, strobe (extreme) on master, plus part of a second batch on
deck B (5 of 9 commands landed before another agent's Himmel stack started
and rewrote the state block header: the "one stack at a time" rule), about
3.5 minutes: 60.0 fps,
CPU 0.45 ms / frame, GPU avg 2.4 ms (max 4.0 ms), **0 late frames after
start-up** (2 during start-up, while the commands loaded clips). Not yet run:
a fullscreen 1080p soak with effects (QA, M12).
