Keyboard: pads 1234 / QWER / ASDF / ZXCV (top row to bottom row) · Shift hold = Shift · Space play/stop · ←→ knob page · ↑↓ master encoder · [] previous/next layer · N output mode · P pad mode · M mute layer.
Mouse: drag knobs and the master encoder up/down or use the scroll wheel. Modifier buttons (Shift, Tempo, Swing, Volume, Select, Scene, Pattern, Duplicate, Erase, Note repeat) latch on click so you can combine them with one mouse. Shift releases after the next action. Strobe is capped while safe limits are on (Shift + All → admin).
