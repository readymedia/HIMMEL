HOW TO PLAY

Pad: drag with the mouse (Shift = light touch), or press Use the real Lightpad and play the block. X and Y move the effect; on most programs Y is how much, as on the real unit. The block's Mode button = HOLD.

PROGRAM/BPM: drag or scroll the right knob (00–99). After TAP/BPM the same knob sets the tempo for 5 s. TAP blinks on the beat.

HOLD keeps the effect at the last position when you let go. PAD MOTION (click the top of the lever): touch to record up to 6 s, let go and it loops. MUTE/FREEZE (press and hold the bottom): freezes the picture.

Memories 1–8: press to recall; hold FUNCTION + a key for 2 s to store. AUDIO on CONTROL SELECT: the pad leaves the video alone.
