HOW TO PLAY

Pad: drag with the mouse (Shift = light touch), or press Use the real Lightpad and play the block. X and Y move the effect; on most programs Y is how much, as on the real unit. The block's Mode button = HOLD.

PROGRAM / BPM: drag or scroll the big knob (00–99). After TAP/BPM the same knob sets the tempo for 5 s.

HOLD keeps the effect at the last position when you let go. PAD MOTION (lever up): touch to record up to 6 s, let go and it loops. MUTE/FREEZE (lever down, hold it): freezes the picture.

Memories 1–8: press to recall; hold FUNCTION + a key for 2 s to store. AUDIO on the left lever: the pad leaves the video alone.
