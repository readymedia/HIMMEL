HOW TO PLAY IT

      Keys: click. INPUT A / B choose the input for each channel; press the lit key again and it blinks
      (black or white background in BK / WH, input dropped in C-K / L-K). HOLD freezes a channel.
      MONITOR SELECT picks what the monitor out shows.

      Crossfader: drag, or scroll over it. FINE: drag up/down or scroll.
      Switches: click a position (FADER CURVE, MODE on the front; NTSC/PAL and POWER on the rear).
      NTSC/PAL is read at power-on, as on the real unit.

      Cables: click an input jack to pull or plug its cable. An input without a signal shows blue.

      The real KF4 has no data port, so these are Himmel's own events, not device bytes.
      Positions and a few behaviours are guesses until checked on the owner's unit (see weapons/kf4.md).
