1 · Pad hits → velocity

Press Record 10 hits, then hit one pad 10 times: 3 soft, 4 medium, 3 hard (not Pad 12). Each curve is one hit, pressure over the first 150 ms.

2 · Knob resolution

Pick a knob, press Start, turn it exactly one full turn clockwise (use a mark on the knob), press Stop. Do Knob 1 and the master encoder.

3 · USB timing

Just runs. For the controls report, turn a knob for a few seconds.

4 · Reconnect

Pull the USB cable, wait 3 s, plug it back. The log shows how long reopening took.

Screens (the panel shows exactly what is sent to the MK2)

Demo: hit pads for rings, Knob 1 = speed. Snake and maze: pads 10 / 5 / 6 / 7 = up / left / down / right (they light blue), Play restarts. In the 3D maze, pad pressure = speed. Star Swarm: d-pad (10 / 5 / 6 / 7) or Knob 1 / Knob 2 fly over the left screen, right-column pads fire (red). Munch Maze: eat all dots, big dots scare the hunters. Sky Shield: each knob aims the turret under it, pads 1-8 fire turret 1-8, pads 13-16 fire the last aimed one; Shift + knob sets how far it shoots (see the crosshair).
On the MK2: hold Shift and turn the master encoder to open the mode menu, turn to choose, push to start.

How to check the map

Press, turn or hit every control on the MK2. The matching control on screen should light up, and the LED on the device should sit on the button you hold. A green dot means "seen".
Wrong control lights up? Click the right one on screen, then press the real one - the map is updated and saved. Esc cancels. A dashed red outline means the control is not in the map yet.
