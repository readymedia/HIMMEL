Camera mapper - let a webcam watch the NuVJ screen and map every segment by itself
  1. Point a webcam straight at the NuVJ's screen (close, sharp, no glare), pick it and press Camera on.
  2. Click the 4 corners of the blue glass in the camera picture: top-left, top-right, bottom-right, bottom-left. Green outlines should then sit on the real segments. Not quite? Drag the orange corners until they do (Alt + arrow keys nudge the last one by a pixel).
  3. Press Auto-map and leave it (about 4-5 minutes). The NuVJ's text row stays blank meanwhile.

Host (the live view standing in for Himmel) · LEDs

Himmel: what Himmel would light: latch / auto / sync / master FX / insta black as toggles, the playing pad per deck, BPM Tap blinking on the beat. Follow: a LED lights while you hold its button. LEDs and LCD are sent as the NuVJ's own feedback SysEx (F0 00 01 3F 7F 20 …, read from the NuVJ 1.0 software); the drawing shows what the device was sent.

Real NuVJ: probe, map and LEDs

Plug the NuVJ in with USB and close the NuVJ / GrandVJ software (Windows lets one program use a MIDI port). Press Use the real NuVJ. Himmel then sends the same init, LED and LCD messages as the NuVJ software (never the stored-contrast command).
Off = listen only: nothing is sent to the NuVJ.

1 · Touch every control

The drawing follows the real NuVJ. A green dot = seen on the hardware. A dashed red outline = not in the map. Wrong control moves? Click the right one in the drawing, then move the real one (turn endless knobs and jog wheels clockwise; walk a switch through all its positions). The map is saved to nuvj_map.json (backup .bak.json). Esc cancels.

2 · LEDs

Try All on and Chase above, and Follow while you press buttons and pads: the LED under your finger must light (31 LEDs: 18 pads, LATCH / AUTO / SYNC ×2, SCRATCH ×2, INSTA A/B, MASTER FX, INSTA BLACK, BPM TAP).

3 · LCD

The LCD is a custom segment display: 8 dot-matrix characters (command 01) and 64 bytes of segment memory (command 02). From the NuVJ software we know the text and where its 2-digit number goes (drawn as the big digits). The rest (small digits, bar, icons) is learned with the segment hunt: it lights one segment bit at a time and writes its number on the text row (e.g. SEG23.4). Click the part of the drawn LCD that lit up (a digit segment, a bar block, an icon or a label) and it is saved in the map. Nothing lit? Step on. Auto steps every 1.2 s.
