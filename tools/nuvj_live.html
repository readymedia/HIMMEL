Himmel: what Himmel would light: latch / auto / sync / master FX / insta black as toggles, the playing pad per deck, BPM Tap blinking on the beat. Follow: a LED lights while you hold its button. The LEDs are driven with note-on messages (the button's own note); this is the first guess for the real NuVJ, checked with the LED probe below.

Real NuVJ: probe, map and LEDs

Plug the NuVJ in with USB and close the NuVJ / GrandVJ software (Windows lets one program use a MIDI port). Press Use the real NuVJ. The probe only listens until the host sends LED notes.

1 · Touch every control

The drawing follows the real NuVJ. A green dot = seen on the hardware. A dashed red outline = not in the map. Wrong control moves? Click the right one in the drawing, then move the real one (turn endless knobs and jog wheels clockwise; walk a switch through all its positions). The map is saved to nuvj_map.json (backup .bak.json). Esc cancels.
